This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "とある王の勇者冒険譚" (hereinafter "App") provided by the operator of とある王の勇者冒険譚 (hereinafter "Provider"). The App is provided on an AS IS basis.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App does not require user registration and does not collect any personally identifiable information, such as names, email addresses, contacts, or location information. However, only if the user has agreed in the device settings to share information with Apple, the following limited technical information may be shared with the Provider by Apple through the standard features of the operating system:

  • Device type
  • OS version
  • Information about app stability (such as crash logs)

This information is used solely to fix defects in and improve the quality of the App.

2. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that send user information externally, such as advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases. If any of these are introduced in the future, the Provider will revise this Privacy Policy and clearly state what information is collected and where it is sent.

3. Data Storage

The App stores game progress (play records) and settings only on the user's device. Information displayed in the Home Screen widget is only shared between the App and the widget on the same device. This data is never sent to or stored on any external server, including the Provider's. If the App is uninstalled, or if records are erased from the App's settings, the related data is deleted and cannot be restored by the Provider.

4. Notifications

Only if the user allows notifications, the App schedules and displays in-game notices (for example, when the storehouse is full) on the device. Notifications are scheduled only on the device, and no information is sent externally. Notifications can be turned off at any time from the App or the device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personally identifiable information from users of any age. The same applies to information about children.

6. Security

The Provider strives to protect user information. The App uses the security mechanisms provided by the operating system to ensure the safety of information processed on the device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as necessary. Any changes will be posted in the App and on the Provider's website, and the last updated date will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of October 10, 2026.

9. Consent

By using the App, users are deemed to have consented to this Privacy Policy.
