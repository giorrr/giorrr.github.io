1. Information Collection and Use

The App does not require user registration and does not directly collect any personally identifiable information from users. However, because the App displays advertisements, a third-party advertising service collects technical information (see Section 3).

Based on the user's explicit actions, the App uses the following device features:

  • Camera: Used to capture and import paper sheet music, and to record videos of the user's own performances.
  • Microphone: Used only when recording performance videos with sound. The recorded audio is saved on the device as part of the video and is never transmitted.
  • Photos: The iOS photo picker is used to select images of sheet music or covers, and performance videos to compare. The App only reads the images and videos the user selects.
  • Files: The iOS file picker is used to import sheet music PDFs and images. The App only reads the files the user selects.
  • Speech: The speech synthesis feature built into iOS is used to read note names aloud.
  • Live Activities: While practice time is being tracked, elapsed time and related information are shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

All data, including imported sheet music images, sheet music recognition results, practice records, recorded performance videos, settings, and Premium Plan subscription status, is processed and stored only on the device and is never sent to the Provider or any third party. Sheet music recognition is also performed on the device. The App does not use location information (precise location) or contacts.

The App communicates externally in the following cases:

  • When loading and displaying advertisements (see Section 3)
  • When purchasing or restoring the Premium Plan, and when checking subscription status (the App communicates with Apple's App Store; see Section 8)
  • When downloading piano sounds (sound data is received from Apple's servers)
  • When opening the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or contact page within the App (the Provider's web pages or a Google Form are loaded)

2. Information Shared by Apple

If the user has enabled "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, some crash and other information collected by Apple may be shared with the Provider through Apple. This information is handled in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy, and the Provider uses it solely to fix defects in the App.

For details on how Apple handles information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apart from the advertising service (see Section 3), the App does not include any third-party services (SDKs) for usage analytics, crash collection, or similar purposes.

3. Advertising Service (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, for the purpose of displaying advertisements. While the user is subscribed to the Premium Plan (see Section 8), no ads are displayed. Google AdMob may collect information such as the following in order to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and display ads based on users' interests:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA; only if the user has allowed tracking)
  • IP address and the approximate location estimated from it
  • Device information, such as device type, OS version, and language
  • Information about interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information about ad behavior (crashes, performance)

Permission to Track (App Tracking Transparency)

The App uses the iOS tracking permission dialog to ask users for permission to use the advertising identifier. Even if the user does not allow tracking, all features of the App remain available. In that case, the advertising identifier is not used, and ads that are not based on the user's interests are displayed. This permission can be changed at any time in the device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

Ad Content and Handling of Information

The Provider cannot control the information collected by Google AdMob. Google handles information in accordance with its own privacy policy. For details on how Google handles information, please refer to the following policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. For Users in the EU, EEA, UK, and Switzerland (Rights Under the GDPR)

This section applies to users residing in the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland.

Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

The processing of personal data for advertising purposes in the App is based on the user's consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). The App requests consent through the consent management platform provided by Google AdMob (UMP: User Messaging Platform) at first launch or as needed. Even if the user does not consent, the features of the App remain available, and non-personalized ads may be displayed.

Transfers of Data Outside the EU/EEA

Google AdMob may transfer data to servers outside the EU/EEA (mainly in the United States). Such transfers are carried out on the basis of appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

Your Rights (Articles 15–22 GDPR)

Users residing in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland have the following rights:

  • Right of access: The right to request access to the personal data being processed
  • Right to rectification: The right to request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten): The right to request the deletion of personal data under certain conditions
  • Right to restriction of processing: The right to request the restriction of the processing of personal data under certain conditions
  • Right to data portability: The right to receive the personal data provided in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: The right to object to the processing of personal data for certain purposes
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in the country of residence

Withdrawing or Changing Consent

Consent to advertising can be changed or withdrawn at any time from [Privacy Settings] in the App's "Settings". Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Requests to Exercise Your Rights

For requests regarding the exercise of the above rights, please contact us via the contact form at the end of this page. Please note that, because the Provider does not hold personal data of App users, for data collected and processed by Google AdMob, please follow Google's guidance (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

5. Data Storage

The App does not store user data on the Provider's servers. All data handled by the App is kept on the user's device, and its management is entrusted to the device's operating system. If device backups (such as iCloud Backup) are enabled, App data is included in backups according to the standard behavior of the operating system. However, recorded or selected performance videos are not included in backups because of their large size (the videos will be lost if the user moves to a new device or deletes the App). If the App is uninstalled, related data will be deleted according to the standard behavior of the device.

6. Children's Privacy

The App can be used by people of all ages. The Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from any user, including children. The information collected by the advertising service is as described in Section 3. Parents or guardians can choose not to allow tracking in the device settings when their children use the App.

7. Security

The Provider strives to protect user information. The App uses the security mechanisms provided by the operating system (Data Protection) to ensure the safety of information processed on the device.

8. In-App Purchases (Premium Plan)

The App offers a paid Premium Plan (auto-renewable subscription). Purchases, payments, renewals, and cancellations are processed by Apple's App Store, and the Provider does not obtain payment information (such as credit card numbers), names, email addresses, or Apple IDs.

To determine whether the Premium Plan is available, the App receives subscription status information from Apple through StoreKit, such as the subscribed plan, the expiration date, and purchase transaction identifiers, and uses it only on the device. This information is never sent to the Provider or any third party. For details on how Apple handles information, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy referenced in Section 2.

If the Provider adds features in the future that involve the collection of new information, such as integration with external services, the Provider will revise this Privacy Policy in advance and clearly state what information is collected and for what purpose.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the last updated date and effective date will be updated accordingly. Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of October 27, 2026.

11. Consent

By using the App, users are deemed to have consented to this Privacy Policy. Users residing in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland may withdraw their consent to advertising at any time, as described in Section 4.
